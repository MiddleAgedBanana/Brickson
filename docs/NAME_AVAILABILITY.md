name reservation or legal clearance. The owner has chosen to retain Brickson.

## Relevant existing names

- [Brixon on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/brixon/id6762793013)
  is a fabric sourcing/business app. It is a different spelling with a
  similar pronunciation, and warrants consideration in a clearance search.
- [Brixon](https://brixon.app/en/brixon-for-the-field) also names a software
  assistant for field technicians.
- [Brickson by Typefactory](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/brickson-font-typefactory)
  is an existing font product.

These uses do not by themselves establish whether the AAC app infringes
any rights. An empty exact-name search does not settle that question either.

## Earlier research supplied by the owner

The owner supplied Claude's `name-and-account.pdf`, researched October 10
and updated October 12, 2026. Its pages 3–6 report a wider Apple search across
176 storefronts, no exact US or Australian mark, and several similar marks,
including BRIXON. It reports that `.com` was parked for sale and `.app` and
`.org` looked unregistered. These wider results and any quoted prices were
not independently reproduced here; EU, UK, Canadian and international
clearance remains outstanding. Its account/category recommendations are
separate research, not new repository decisions or instructions to register
anything. The original PDF is not published with this repository.

## Before launch

1. Confirm the chosen title in the intended App Store Connect account.
   [Apple's app-record instructions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-a-new-app/)
   explain name use. Public searches cannot confirm a private reservation.
2. Complete similar-mark and common-law checks for the intended markets.
   [USPTO clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks)
   and [WIPO's database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index)
   describe the wider search. No trademark application has been filed here.
3. If a custom domain is wanted, verify `brickson.app` at a registrar and
   review first-year and renewal prices before purchase. Existing GitHub
   Pages support/privacy URLs remain usable; do not rename the repository.
4. Check pronunciation with VoiceOver, Siri and Voice Control on real devices.
   Social handles are optional and have not been reserved or verified.
