Brickson Privacy Policy

Effective [Effective Date]

The short version: Brickson keeps everything you put in it on your own iPhone or iPad. We never receive your phrases, your voice or anything you type, so we cannot see them, sell them or share them. Brickson has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

Brickson is a free communication app made by [Developer Name]. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean [Developer Name]. You can reach us at [Contact Email].

What stays on your device

Brickson saves these things on your device, in storage that only Brickson can open:

  • The phrases and categories you use, add or change.
  • The message you are typing, so it is still there if the app closes. It stays until you clear it.
  • Your settings, such as the voice, speaking speed and text size you chose.

iOS keeps other apps out of this storage, and encrypts it when your device has a passcode. It stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of it from the device.

Speech and your Personal Voice

When you choose a phrase, Brickson hands the words to the speech system built into iOS, and your device speaks them. Brickson does not record what it says, and it does not send your words anywhere.

Personal Voice is an Apple feature, not part of Brickson. You create it in the Settings app, and Apple keeps it encrypted on your device. If you turn on Share Across Devices, Apple also keeps an end-to-end encrypted copy in iCloud. Brickson can speak in your Personal Voice only if you allow it. When you do:

  • Brickson gives iOS the words, and iOS says them in your voice.
  • Brickson never receives your recordings or the voice itself. Apple does not let apps capture speech made with a Personal Voice.
  • You can turn this off any time in Settings > Accessibility > Personal Voice.

Your Personal Voice belongs to iOS, so deleting Brickson leaves it in place. Apple explains how it protects Personal Voice in Create a Personal Voice.

How you control the app

Brickson works with Apple's accessibility features, such as Eye Tracking, Switch Control and Voice Control. iOS runs these features. Brickson never receives camera images, eye-tracking data or anything else they use; it only learns which button you chose.

Backups

Backups you export. If you export a backup, Brickson makes a file with your categories and phrases. You choose where it goes: the Files app, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email or another app. From then on the file is yours, and wherever you saved it looks after it under its own rules. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who has it can read your phrases. Keep it somewhere private, and delete copies you no longer need.

Your device's own backups. If iCloud Backup is on, your Brickson data is included in your iCloud backup, which Apple encrypts. A backup to a computer is encrypted only if you choose Encrypt local backup. Apple or your computer handles these backups, not us.

If you turn on recorded phrases

Brickson uses the microphone only while you record a phrase, and only after you allow it. While it records, Brickson shows that it is recording, and iOS shows its own indicator. Recordings are saved on your device with your phrases and are included in backups you export. They are never sent to us. You can turn off microphone access in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

If you turn on caregiver alerts

Caregiver alerts are off until you turn them on. When they are on, Get Attention also alerts Brickson on a caregiver's iPhone or iPad over your home Wi-Fi.

  • iOS asks your permission before Brickson can use your local network.
  • The alert says that you need attention, and shows the name you chose for your device.
  • It goes straight from your device to the caregiver's device, encrypted. It never goes over the internet, and we cannot see it.
  • To stop, turn caregiver alerts off in Brickson, or turn off Brickson in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.

If you turn on Siri and Shortcuts

You can ask Siri to say one of your phrases, or use your phrases in the Shortcuts app. To make this work, Brickson lets iOS see the names of your phrases. When you use Siri, it may send your request, and details such as shortcut names, to Apple. Apple handles that under its Siri privacy policy. We never receive it.

What we never collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health records or camera images.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking across other apps and websites. Because nothing tracks you, browser Do Not Track settings have nothing to change.
  • No code from other companies that could collect data.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we never receive any.

What Apple shares with us

Apple runs the App Store, and under its own privacy policy it may share limited information with us:

  • Crash reports and usage statistics, only if you turned on Share With App Developers in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. Crash reports describe what went wrong in the app and your device model and iOS version; they do not include your phrases. We use them only to fix problems.
  • Totals such as downloads and ratings, which do not identify you.
  • Test versions. If you test Brickson through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares with us your name and email address (if we invited you by email), details about your device, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you send. We use them only to improve Brickson, never share them, and delete feedback when the test ends.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems, and we never share it. Please leave out medical details unless we need them to help. We delete support emails within one year after the conversation ends, or sooner if you ask.

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Brickson is not directed to children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone, including children. If a child emails us, we reply once and then delete the email.

Your choices

  • Every optional feature above can stay off; the phrase board works without them.
  • Delete a phrase or category in Brickson, and it is gone from the app.
  • Delete Brickson to remove everything it stored on the device.
  • Delete any backup files you exported, wherever you saved them.
  • Change any permission in the Settings app at any time.

Emergencies

Emergency phrases and Get Attention make sound on your device. They do not call emergency services, and unless you turn on caregiver alerts, they do not contact anyone.
